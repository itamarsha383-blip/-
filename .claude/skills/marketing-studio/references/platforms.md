# Platform playbooks

Each platform rewards a different shape of content. Read the section for the user's
priority platform in Phase 4 and honor the micro-specs — they are the difference
between "fine" and "world-class". When a user posts everywhere, design for the
priority platform first, then adapt.

## Instagram Reels
The main growth engine on Instagram — this is where non-followers discover you.
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16 (1080×1920). Keep text out of the top ~250px and bottom
  ~350px "safe zone" — the UI (username, caption, buttons) covers it.
- **Length**: 7–30s for pure reach; 30–60s when depth builds saves/shares. Shorter
  usually wins for a cold audience.
- **First frame**: must contain a visual hook + on-screen text. No slow intros, no
  logos, no "hey guys". The first 1 second decides the watch.
- **Retention**: cut or change the frame every ~1.5–2s (new angle, zoom, text pop,
  b-roll). Momentum keeps people watching; a static talking head loses them.
- **Loop**: if the last frame flows back into the first, it replays and inflates
  watch time.
- **Captions**: burn subtitles into the video (most watch on mute). The written
  caption's first line must hook before the "...more" cutoff (~125 chars).
- **CTA**: one, specific — "comment X for the link", "save this for later", "follow
  for part 2". Saves and shares are worth more than likes to the algorithm.
- **Hashtags**: 3–5 relevant ones, not 30. Put them in the caption or first comment.
- **Cadence**: consistency beats volume; 4–7 Reels/week is a strong growth pace.

## Instagram carousel / feed post
Best format for saves, depth, and "value" content.
- **Size**: 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait — takes more screen than square).
- **Slide 1** is a mini-billboard: a bold headline that promises a payoff and makes
  people swipe. Big readable text, high contrast.
- **Slides 2–N**: one idea per slide, minimal text, a visual rhythm. End on a
  summary slide + CTA slide ("save this", "follow @handle").
- **Caption**: hooky first line, then value, line breaks for skimmability, a CTA.
- 7–10 slides is a strong default for a "how-to" or list.

## TikTok
Fastest discovery on any platform; native, raw, fast content wins over polished.
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16, same safe zones as Reels but the right-side UI is wider.
- **Length**: 9–34s for reach; longer (60s+) can work for storytelling with a strong
  hook and rewards watch-time.
- **Hook**: spoken AND on-screen within the first 1–2s. TikTok audiences are the most
  ruthless scrollers — open on the most interesting moment, not the setup.
- **Native feel**: talk to camera, trending sounds, jump cuts, text overlays. Overly
  produced ads get scrolled.
- **CTA / comment bait**: leave an intentional open loop or mild "wrong" detail —
  comments and duets are rocket fuel.
- **Cadence**: high frequency rewarded; 1–3/day is common for growth pushes.

## YouTube Shorts
Great for reach that can spill into long-form subscribers.
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16, up to 60s (now up to 3 min, but <60s still favored for reach).
- **Hook**: title text on screen + a spoken promise in the first 2s.
- **Retention** and **loopability** are the ranking signals — same craft as Reels.
- Add a real **title** and let the first line of the description carry keywords.

## LinkedIn
B2B, personal brand, and professional authority. Text posts and documents dominate.
- **Hook**: first 2 lines are everything (the "see more" cutoff). Lead with a
  surprising result, a contrarian professional take, or a specific story.
- **Format**: short lines, generous white space, one idea, a lesson or takeaway.
  "Broetry" (one sentence per line) is skimmable and performs.
- **No outbound links in the post body** (the algorithm suppresses them) — put links
  in the first comment.
- **CTA**: ask a genuine question to spark comments; engagement in the first hour
  drives reach.
- **Documents/carousels** (PDF) get strong dwell time — great for frameworks.

## X / Twitter
Speed, opinion, and threads.
- **Hook tweet**: must stand alone and provoke curiosity or agreement. First line of a
  thread promises the payoff of reading all of it.
- **Threads**: one idea per tweet, a clear numbered arc, a strong final tweet with a
  CTA (follow / retweet the first tweet).
- Short, punchy, no fluff. Native images/video lift reach.

## Facebook
Skews older; groups and shares drive reach; longer text and emotional stories work.
- Lead with an emotional or relatable hook. Native video and images over links.
- Strong for community, local business, and shareable stories.

## Email
The only channel you own. Highest intent, best for selling.
- **Subject line** is the hook — curiosity, specificity, or benefit; test 2–3.
- **First line** (preview text) must continue the hook, not restate the subject.
- One email = one idea = one CTA. Write like a human to one person.
- Story → lesson → offer is a reliable structure.
