# Marketing Brief — Novixel.ai

_Last updated: 2026-07-21. Load this at the start of every marketing session._

## The business
**Novixel.ai** (novixelai.com) — an **AI receptionist / lead-response service** for
businesses. It answers calls, web chat, and forms 24/7, books appointments straight
to the calendar, sends instant lead alerts, follows up by SMS, and is trained in the
client's brand voice. Also produces brand videos.

### Offer / pricing
- **Launch — $97/mo**: 24/7 AI answering (web chat & forms), books to calendar,
  instant text + email alert on every lead, one brand video, trained in brand voice.
  _"Best for website, chat & form leads — stop missing them."_
- **Convert — $197/mo (Most Popular)**: everything in Launch + AI voice answering
  real phone calls, fresh cinematic video monthly, SMS follow-up & reminders,
  calendar + CRM integrations, priority onboarding.
  _"Best for phone + SMS + calendar booking — close more."_
- No setup fee, cancel anytime. CTA on site: "Call Novi now".

## Core value / emotional hook
**A missed call is money walking out the door.** Novixel is the employee that never
sleeps, never gets sick, and costs less than $200/month — so you never lose another
lead to voicemail or a faster competitor.

## Target customer
American small & local **service businesses** that lose leads by missing calls/chats:
roofers, contractors, HVAC, med spas, salons, dentists, real estate, agencies, law
firms, auto shops. The buyer is a busy owner who can't answer every call.

## Constraints & preferences
- **Owner stays 100% anonymous** → all content is **faceless** (AI voice, screen
  recordings, text-on-screen, b-roll, demos). This is an advantage, not a limit.
- **Platforms**: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook (+ Threads).
- **Content language**: English (American audience).
- **Goal**: blend of sales + audience — prioritize whatever drives **revenue fastest**.
